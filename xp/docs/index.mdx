# Level Up XP

Level Up XP (XP) adds gamification to Moodle. Learners receive points for their actions. They can see their progress, reach new levels, and compare their progress on a leaderboard.

You can use XP separately in each course or across your Moodle site. You can configure the points system, the levels, and the information that learners see.

## Level Up XP+ {#xp-plus}

Level Up XP+ (XP+) is a paid add-on for XP.

Some features in this documentation require XP+. When a specific plan is required, the notice identifies it.

XP+ adds more ways to award points and gives you more control over leaderboards, reports, and integrations. For example, you can award points for activity completion, create team leaderboards, and export reports.

For a complete and current list, [compare the plans and features on the Level Up website](https://www.levelup.plus/xp/#packages).

## How XP works

1. You define the actions that give points.
2. Learners receive points when they perform these actions.
3. Their point totals determine their levels.
4. The XP block and leaderboards show their progress.

You can also use a learner's level to control access to content or to enrol the learner in another course.
