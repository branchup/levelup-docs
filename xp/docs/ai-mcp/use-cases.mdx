# Ways to use XP with AI

You can speak naturally to your AI assistant. You do not need to know the names
of the Level Up XP tools or find Moodle record IDs yourself.

Be specific about the course, learner or period you are interested in. When a
request could make a change, ask the assistant to review the details with you
first.

## Understand how points are earned

Ask the assistant to combine the XP levels, rules and course structure into a
plain-language summary.

> In Introduction to Psychology, summarise how learners earn points and how
> many points they need for each level. Highlight activities that do not appear
> to have a related reward. Do not make any changes.

You can follow up with questions such as:

- Are some activities rewarded much more heavily than others?
- Which rules have limits?
- Are there completion rewards for the main course activities?

## Review a learner's progress

When access to personal information is allowed, the assistant can find a
learner and review their XP progress.

> Find Jordan Lee in Introduction to Psychology and summarise their current
> points, level and progress towards the next level.

If more than one learner matches the name, the assistant should ask you to
choose rather than guess.

## Investigate recent points

The XP logs can help explain what recently happened.

> Review Jordan Lee's Level Up XP activity in Introduction to Psychology over
> the last seven days. Summarise where their points came from and identify
> repeated awards.

The assistant reports what appears in the XP logs. It cannot determine a
learner's intent, and an unusual pattern is not necessarily misuse.

## Review a leaderboard

The assistant can summarise individual and team standings without requiring
you to export the leaderboard.

> Summarise the top ten positions in the Introduction to Psychology
> leaderboard. Include the gap between each position and the next one.

Only request identifiable learner information when you have a legitimate
reason to use it.

## Get ideas for improving the setup

The assistant can compare the course structure with the Level Up XP features
and rules available to you.

> Review the XP setup and course structure for Introduction to Psychology.
> Suggest three small improvements that use features currently available in
> Level Up XP. Explain why each suggestion may help. Do not make changes.

The assistant can suggest changes that you apply in Moodle, but it cannot
currently edit XP settings, levels or rules for you.

## Award points

Awarding points changes a learner's XP total. First ask the assistant to resolve
the learner and show you the intended action:

> Find Jordan Lee in Introduction to Psychology. Prepare an award of 50 points
> for helping another learner, but do not award the points yet.

After checking the learner, course and amount, give an explicit instruction:

> Award the 50 points now. Do not send a notification.

To notify the learner, say so explicitly and provide the message:

> Award the 50 points now and notify the learner with the message: Thank you
> for helping another learner.

If awarding points is unavailable, your administrator may have disabled tools
that make changes. See the [administrator guide](./admin.mdx#choose-which-tools-are-available).

## Use safer prompts

These phrases help make your intent clear:

- **Do not make any changes** when you only want advice or information.
- **Show me what you intend to do first** before an action.
- Include the exact course and learner when they are known.
- State whether a learner should be notified.
- Ask the assistant to say when it cannot verify something.

Always review important results in Moodle. An AI assistant can help you work
with XP, but it remains your responsibility to decide whether its conclusions
and actions are appropriate.
